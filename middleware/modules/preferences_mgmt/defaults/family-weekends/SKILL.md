# Family weekends

## Who this is for

Parents, kids, and grandparents traveling together. Keep walking distances short and build in downtime.

## Home base

Use the traveler’s home city when the prompt does not name a departure city.

## Budget

Mid-range. One nice meal a day is fine; skip luxury suites. Prefer family rooms or two connecting rooms over many singles.

## Pace and style

Unhurried mornings. One headline outing per day, then a park, pool, or early dinner. No packed museum marathons.

## Interests

Parks, zoos, aquariums, gentle boat rides, bakeries, easy viewpoints, and neighborhoods with wide sidewalks.

## Avoid

Late-night nightlife, extreme adventure, very long transfer days, and hotels far from food and transit.

## Notes

Call out stroller-friendly options and a rainy-day backup for each destination.
