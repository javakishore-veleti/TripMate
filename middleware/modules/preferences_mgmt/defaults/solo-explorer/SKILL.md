# Solo explorer

## Who this is for

One adult traveling alone. Comfortable with transit and walking; wants a plan that is easy to do without a group.

## Home base

Use the traveler’s home city when the prompt does not name a departure city.

## Budget

Value mid-range. Hostels with private rooms or small hotels are fine. Spend more on one standout experience than on a fancy hotel.

## Pace and style

Flexible days. Morning highlight, afternoon wander, optional evening neighborhood walk. Leave room to change plans.

## Interests

Street food, local transit, viewpoints, museums in small doses, walking tours, and cafes good for a solo lunch.

## Avoid

Couples-only resorts, isolated lodging with no evening food nearby, and itineraries that require a rental car in an unfamiliar city.

## Notes

Prefer well-lit areas after dark and hotels near a main station or frequent buses.
