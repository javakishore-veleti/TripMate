# Couple getaway

## Who this is for

Two adults who want time together more than a checklist of sights.

## Home base

Use the traveler’s home city when the prompt does not name a departure city.

## Budget

Mid-range to a little treat. One nicer dinner and a pleasant room matter more than covering every landmark.

## Pace and style

Late starts, long lunches, sunset walks. Two main activities a day at most.

## Interests

Food, wine or tea, waterfronts, small museums, gardens, and a neighborhood that is nice after dark.

## Avoid

Theme-park crowds, backpacker-party hostels, and dawn departures unless the trip is short.

## Notes

Suggest one “memory” booking: a cooking class, a view room, or a short day trip you can skip if the weather turns.
