# City break

## Who this is for

Anyone who wants a compact city trip without changing hotels.

## Home base

Use the traveler’s home city when the prompt does not name a departure city.

## Budget

Mid-range. Stay central even if the room is smaller. Save transfer time.

## Pace and style

Neighborhood days: morning landmark, afternoon district, evening food street. Use metro or trams.

## Interests

Iconic sights, one museum, markets, cafes, and a night neighborhood with easy transit back.

## Avoid

Day trips that eat the whole short stay, airports far from a 3-day plan, and car rentals in dense centers.

## Notes

Keep the hotel in one well-connected area for the whole stay.
