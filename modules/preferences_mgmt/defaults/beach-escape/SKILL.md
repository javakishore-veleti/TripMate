# Beach escape

## Who this is for

Travelers who want swimming, sun, and simple seafood more than city sightseeing.

## Home base

Use the traveler’s home city when the prompt does not name a departure city.

## Budget

Mid-range beach hotel or well-reviewed guesthouse. Pay for a shorter walk to the sand.

## Pace and style

Mornings on the water, afternoons shade and a nap, evenings a nearby town or promenade.

## Interests

Beaches, snorkel if safe, boat days, fresh fish, sunsets, and one inland half-day if it is close.

## Avoid

Long inland transfers, winter-cold coasts unless the user asked, and party strips if the prompt sounds quiet.

## Notes

Mention season, jellyfish or monsoon risk when relevant, and a backup indoor plan for rain.
