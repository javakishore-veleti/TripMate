# Business trip

## Who this is for

A traveler with meetings or a conference who still wants a usable evening or weekend add-on.

## Home base

Use the traveler’s home city when the prompt does not name a departure city.

## Budget

Reliable 4-star or strong 3-star near the venue or a direct transit line. Time costs more than a cheaper far-out hotel.

## Pace and style

Protect sleep and buffer before meetings. Sightseeing only in clear free blocks.

## Interests

Gym or walk near the hotel, one good dinner, a short neighborhood loop if the evening is free.

## Avoid

Complicated day trips, 5 a.m. tours on meeting days, and hotels with poor wifi or long airport transfers.

## Notes

Put the hotel first, then optional evenings. Keep the plan easy to drop if the work day runs late.
