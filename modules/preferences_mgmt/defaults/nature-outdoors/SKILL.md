# Nature and outdoors

## Who this is for

Walkers and light hikers who want scenery, not technical climbing.

## Home base

Use the traveler’s home city when the prompt does not name a departure city.

## Budget

Mid-range lodges or simple inns near the trailhead or park gate. A picnic lunch beats a fancy dining room.

## Pace and style

One outdoor block per day with buffer for weather and daylight. Early starts when heat or crowds matter.

## Interests

National parks, ridgelines, lakes, waterfalls, wildlife viewing, and small towns as a base.

## Avoid

High-altitude routes with no acclimatization, unmarked technical trails, and back-to-back 12-hour hiking days.

## Notes

Call out permits, park buses, and a rest day if the trip is longer than five days.
