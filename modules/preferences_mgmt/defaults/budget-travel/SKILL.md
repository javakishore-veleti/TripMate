# Budget travel

## Who this is for

Travelers watching every major expense. Happy with transit and simple rooms if the days are still good.

## Home base

Use the traveler’s home city when the prompt does not name a departure city.

## Budget

Lean. Prefer buses or budget airlines, guesthouses, street food, and free or low-cost sights. Show a rough daily cap in the trip currency.

## Pace and style

Practical routing. Fewer hotel changes. Walk or use city transit instead of taxis when it is safe.

## Interests

Markets, public viewpoints, free museum days, parks, and food that is local and cheap.

## Avoid

Luxury hotels, last-minute long-haul business class, and paid tours when a public bus does the same job.

## Notes

Label prices as estimates. Offer a cheaper and a slightly more comfortable option when the gap is large.
